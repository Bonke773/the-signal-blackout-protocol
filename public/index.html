FIELD MANUAL

Objective: Restore at least 2 of 3 network nodes before integrity reaches zero.

2–3 players: Everyone cooperates. 4–8 players: Secret infiltrators try to prevent restoration.

Every operation: 40 seconds to solve a puzzle and choose REPAIR or SCAN. Infiltrators may instead SABOTAGE. A node is repaired when at least half of active players solve correctly and one correct solver chooses REPAIR.

Investigation: Review true but incomplete system logs. In infiltration mode, vote to quarantine a suspect or SKIP. A strict majority is needed; incorrect quarantine costs 10 integrity.

Scoring: Correct answers +100 personal points, successful repairs +40, sabotage +60. Score is bragging rights; the match winner is a team.

Timer: Decisions lock automatically at phase end. No chat or microphone needed—discuss in person or via your preferred voice call.

Tip: A suspicious clue is not necessarily proof. Even infiltrators can solve puzzles.
